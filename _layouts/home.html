AetheRock: An Arm-Worn Robot Teaching System for Force-Guided Vision-Tactile Learning

An arm-worn robot teaching system that collects gripper force, vision, and tactile data for contact-rich manipulation. Its ForceVT framework uses force and vision to guide tactile representation learning, improving robustness to sensor manufacturing and usage differences.
